🇵🇰 Muhammad Ali Jinnah 
Muhammad Ali Jinnah (1876–1948), commonly known as Quaid-e-
Azam (“Great Leader”), was a lawyer, politician, and the principal leader of the 
All-India Muslim League during the struggle that resulted in the creation of 
Pakistan in 1947. 
  Basic information 
• Full name: Muhammad Ali Jinnah 
• Born: 25 December 1876, Karachi 
• Died: 11 September 1948, Karachi 
• Profession: Barrister/lawyer and politician 
• Political organization: All-India Muslim League 
• Position: First Governor-General of Pakistan 
• Title: Quaid-e-Azam 
• Country: Pakistan 🇵🇰 
  What did he do? 
Jinnah initially worked for Hindu-Muslim political cooperation and was 
involved in the Indian National Congress as well as the Muslim League. Over 
time, however, he became increasingly convinced that Muslims in British India 
needed stronger political safeguards and eventually supported the creation of a 
separate Muslim state. 
The Lahore Resolution of 1940 was a major turning point in the Pakistan 
movement. The Muslim League subsequently campaigned for a separate 
homeland, and after negotiations, political conflict, and the end of British 
rule, Pakistan was created on 14 August 1947. 
Jinnah became Pakistan's first Governor-General. 
  His leadership style 
One interesting thing about Jinnah is that he was known for being extremely 
disciplined and controlled. 
He was a trained lawyer, and his political style often emphasized: 
• Preparation 

• Logical arguments 
• Discipline 
• Persistence 
• Organization 
• Self-confidence 
• Constitutional and legal methods 
He could be very firm when he believed something was important.
